About Us

Building the future of enterprise training.

BulletTrain combines conversational AI with immersive avatar technology to help franchise teams practice the moments that matter before they matter.

Our Mission

Training that actually changes behavior.

We believe the best training is experiential. Not a video. Not a quiz. A real conversation, real stakes, and real feedback.

Most training programs stop at knowledge transfer. BulletTrain goes one step further: we put people inside the conversation they need to master and score them on it. That gap between knowing and doing is exactly where we operate.

Franchise operators built this with us. Every scenario in the library was designed alongside the people who run the floor every day.

  • Scenario-first: every simulation starts with a real business moment.
  • Evidence-based feedback: AI analysis tied directly to what was said and when.
  • Built for scale: deploy across a franchise system without adding headcount.
