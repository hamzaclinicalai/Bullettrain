Monitor Training Across Multiple Locations

Deploy scenarios. Set the bar. Require the test.

Assign practice scenarios to any team across your organization. When they are ready, require them to pass a graded test and meet your performance threshold before they step onto the floor.

  • Assign practice scenarios - staff warm up with guided reps at their own pace. The avatar coaches; no trainer required.
  • Require the blind test - managers set a minimum score threshold. Staff must pass before they are cleared for the floor.
  • See who is ready - objective-by-objective scores and AI coaching notes surface exactly where each person stands. No guesswork.
AI Analysis

A debrief that tells the truth.

The analysis engine scores each session against the simulation's learning objectives, not a generic rubric, and generates trainee-specific coaching language.

Objective-level scoring

Each learning objective gets its own score so a trainee knows exactly which skill moved the needle and which needs another rep.

Talk-time analysis

Who dominated the conversation? Talk-time imbalance is one of the strongest predictors of missed discovery and rapport. The platform surfaces it instantly.

Evidence-backed coaching

Every coaching note links directly to the moment in the transcript where the skill gap appeared. No guesswork for the manager or the trainee.
