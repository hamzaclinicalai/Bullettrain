Built for enterprise L&D teams

Deploy scenarios. Set the bar. Require the test.

Assign practice scenarios to any team across your organization. When they are ready, require them to pass a graded test and meet your performance threshold before they step onto the floor.

  • Assign practice scenarios — staff warm up with guided reps at their own pace. The avatar coaches; no trainer required.
  • Require the blind test — managers set a minimum score threshold. Staff must pass before they are cleared for the floor.
  • See who is ready — objective-by-objective scores and AI coaching notes surface exactly where each person stands. No guesswork.
Platform

From scenario to skill in a single session.

Three phases. One cohesive experience. No trainers required on repeat visits.

Scenario-first library

Every simulation is built around a real franchise moment: a service recovery, an objection, a coaching conversation. Not a generic script.

Practice then Test

Trainees warm up in practice mode with light guidance, then unlock test mode for a blind run. Skills are locked in, not just demonstrated once.

AI performance analysis

After every session the platform grades each objective, shows the trainee exactly where they succeeded or stumbled, and recommends their next rep.
