AI performance analysis

Dual-mode learning

Same scenario. Two completely different experiences.

Practice mode coaches while the trainee runs. Test mode reveals who is truly ready for the floor.

  • Practice mode - The avatar offers a soft nudge when the trainee goes silent. No grade. No stress. Just reps.
  • Test mode - The avatar stays fully in character. Every turn is recorded, graded, and surfaced in the post-session report.
  • AI debrief - Rubric-scored results tied directly to each learning objective. Managers see aggregate trends; trainees see personal gaps.
